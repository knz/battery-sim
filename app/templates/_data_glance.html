{# _data_glance.html — the "Your data at a glance" band body as a REUSABLE macro (specs/02-ux-wireframes.md §2.3a). A single `data_glance` macro so BOTH places that show these figures render identical content from one source: * _panel_data.html — inside panel ①, between the Data-quality card and the "Next: parameters" CTA, over the dataset's full coverage.
