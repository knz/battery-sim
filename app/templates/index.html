{# ── Source-picker drawer (§2.2 slot-first). A single right-side panel, shared by every slot row's "Choose source…" button. Opened by ha_fetch.js, which fills #drawer-slot-title and #drawer-source-list for the clicked slot. Hidden by default (aria-hidden + off-canvas); closable via the ✕ control, the backdrop, and Escape. The source options and the CSV pending option are rendered into #drawer-source-list by JS from the row's data-* payload; the pending [?] reuses the shared #pending-dialog above (data-feature-key="data_source_csv"). When the selected source is Home Assistant, the drawer also reveals a single entity (statistic id) is (re)populated per slot when the drawer opens or the HA radio is picked: energy statistic ids for energy slots, mean ids for price slots, from the shared connection's listing (ha_fetch.js). Until the connection is tested it shows a disabled hint; the note below points at the connection card. The chosen id is kept in per-slot JS state, not in this DOM node. #}
